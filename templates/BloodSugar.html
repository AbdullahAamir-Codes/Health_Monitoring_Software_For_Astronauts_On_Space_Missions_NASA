Blood sugar, also known as blood glucose, is the amount of glucose present in the bloodstream. Glucose is the body's primary source of energy and plays an essential role in maintaining normal brain function, muscle activity and overall health.

For astronauts travelling in space, maintaining healthy blood sugar levels is particularly important. Microgravity, changes in physical activity, altered sleep patterns, stress and dietary changes can influence how the body processes glucose.

Monitoring blood sugar levels helps astronauts understand their metabolic health, identify unusual changes early and maintain their physical performance throughout long-duration space missions.

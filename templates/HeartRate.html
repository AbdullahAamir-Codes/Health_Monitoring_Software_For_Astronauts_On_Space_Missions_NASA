Why To Use Heart Rate Feature In Space

Space is an extraordinary environment, but it places unusual demands on the human body. Without Earth's gravity, the cardiovascular system must adapt to changes in blood distribution and circulation. Continuous heart rate monitoring can help identify important changes in an astronaut's cardiovascular activity.
